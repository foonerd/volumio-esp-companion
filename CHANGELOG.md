# Changelog

All notable changes to Volumio ESP Companion are recorded here. The format follows [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/). Versions follow [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/spec/v2.0.0.html).

## [Unreleased]

- `firmware/waveshare-esp32-c5-touch-lcd-28`: the board brought up (`src/board.rs`) in the vendor BSP's order through `ch32v003-expander`, mipidsi's ST7789 over SPI2 and `cst3530` on the shared I2C bus, and `companion-runtime`'s `App` run on the panel: status page, face, touch, with the backlight lit after the first frame. Album art and audio not yet. App 1.6 MB in a 3 MB factory partition. Not yet seen on a board.
- `platform/esp-idf`: `Http` over `esp_http_client` with the certificate bundle, `NvsStore` over NVS (a key over 15 bytes is refused, not truncated), `WifiNet` wrapping the blocking Wi-Fi as `NetworkStatus`. No `unsafe`; built inside the firmware's workspace. The firmware joins the network through it, reads its settings from the NVS namespace `companion` and asks the player for `/status`.
- `runtime`: the settings keys respelled to fit NVS's 15-byte names (`text_portrait`, `gap_portrait`, `strip_portrait`, `debounce_ms` and their landscape pairs); `key::ALL` and `key::MAX_KEY_LEN`, with a test holding every key to the limit. Nothing had shipped under the old spellings.
- `drivers/ch32v003-expander`: the IO expander on Waveshare's ESP32 touch panels (eight GPIO, PWM, ADC at `0x24`), from Waveshare's register map and BSP sequences, with a shadow of the write-only registers. `drivers/cst3530`: the touch controller at `0x58`, implementing `TouchSource`, from Waveshare's `esp_lcd_touch_cst3530` component. Both `no_std`, over embedded-hal 1, tested against embedded-hal-mock, built for bare-metal RISC-V in `check.sh`. Neither has met a board yet.
- `sim`: the application on a desktop. `ureq` for the player, the host's interfaces for the status page, the command line as the settings store, a thread for album art (JPEG and PNG through `image`), SDL2 for the panel and the finger behind a `window` feature; `--snapshot` renders to a PNG without a window or a library. Verified against a live Volumio in both orientations. `check.sh` runs the headless shape and keeps the PNG; CI uploads it.
- `runtime`: the application, carried over from rpi-waveshare28 `main.rs`, `input.rs` and the fetching halves of `state.rs`, `art.rs`, `http.rs` and `config.rs` at `61ecf6d`. The Pi's loop, threads and channels become a state machine a device steps with its own clock: `App::touch`, `App::step`, `App::art_request` / `App::art_arrived`. The gesture derivation, the Volumio REST client, the album-art gate with its backoff, the settings keys and the request-target encoding come with their tests (37). `no_std` with `alloc`; builds for bare-metal RISC-V, which `scripts/check.sh` now proves for it too.
- `core`: `PlayerState::parse`; `StatusText`, `BarGap` and `Strip` gain the `as_str` / `parse` pair `Theme` had, for the settings store.
- `hal`: the traits a device implements (touch source, HTTP GET, config store, network status, player discovery, frame present) and the capabilities record a firmware declares as a const. `no_std` with `alloc`.
- `core`: the pages, the transient surfaces, the player state model and the Volumio command shapes, carried over from rpi-waveshare28 at `61ecf6d` with their 48 tests. `no_std` with `alloc`; the fetching and the settings stay in the runtime. Builds for bare-metal RISC-V, which `scripts/check.sh` now proves.
- `firmware/waveshare-esp32-c5-touch-lcd-28`: the first firmware, std Rust on ESP-IDF v5.5.6. On boot it reports the chip model and revision, the PSRAM and heap sizes, a dual-band Wi-Fi scan with each access point's band, channel, strength and auth, an optional connection when credentials were given at build time, and a heap heartbeat every five seconds. No panel, touch or audio yet.
- `core` and `hal`: the two shared crates laid down as enforced contracts (`no_std`, no `unsafe`, no platform crate), empty of code until the port.
- `scripts/check.sh`: format, clippy, tests and docs on the host workspace, the scripts, then every firmware crate built; refuses to pass without the ESP toolchain. `scripts/image.sh`: the merged image and its parts into `dist/` from the offsets ESP-IDF writes to `flasher_args.json`.
